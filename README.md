# Prachi Gupta, PhD

### Cancer Biologist | Immuno-Oncology | Tumor Microenvironment | Translational Cancer Research

PhD-trained cancer biologist with 7+ years of postdoctoral research experience in cancer biology, immuno-oncology, tumor microenvironment, and translational research.

My research focuses on understanding tumor–immune interactions, mechanisms of immune suppression, therapeutic target validation, and the development of preclinical models to advance cancer research.

## 🧬 Research Experience

### Earle A. Chiles Research Institute — Portland, Oregon
**Research Fellow | 2024–Present**

Research focus: Role of ARF6-GTP in tumor-cell intrinsic defense and repair mechanisms that contribute to resistance to T-cell attack.

### Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center — New York
**Research Associate | 2023–2024**

Research focus: Development of patient-specific antigenic T cells for renal cell carcinoma using immunogenic cell death, including ccRCC organoid models and clinical mouse models.

### Medical College of Wisconsin — Wisconsin
**Postdoctoral Fellow | 2019–2023**

Research focus: Role of SPHK1 and sphingosine-1-phosphate signaling in the immunosuppressive tumor microenvironment of ovarian cancer, including extracellular vesicles, immune regulation, and therapeutic target investigation.
